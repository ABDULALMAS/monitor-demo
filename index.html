// FAQ

Questions, answered

How does Acme Analytics track a competitor's website?

You give us a URL, and our crawlers check it on a schedule you control, from once an hour to once a day. Any meaningful change to pricing, copy, or layout is flagged automatically.

Can I try Acme Analytics before paying?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day trial with full feature access and no credit card required. You can add your first competitors in under two minutes.

What happens if I go over my plan's competitor limit?

We'll let you know as you approach your limit and pause new additions rather than cutting off existing tracking. Upgrading takes effect immediately and prorates automatically.

Does Acme Analytics integrate with Slack?

Yes, along with Microsoft Teams and email. You can route different alert types to different channels, so pricing changes and messaging changes don't have to land in the same place.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your account settings with no cancellation fee. You'll keep access through the end of your current billing period.
